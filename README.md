# Sales-Performance-Dashboard

## Overview
Analyzed 51,290 global sales records to identify key business insights using Python.

## Business questions answered
-Which product category generates the highest revenue?
-Which regions are the most profitable for the business?
-Which month drives the highest sales volume throughout the year?
-Which specific product sub-categories contribute most to total revenue?
-Which customer segment delivers the best sales and profit performance?
-Does offering higher discounts actually increase or decrease profitability?

## Tools used
- Python, Pandas, Matplotlib

## Key insights
-Technology is the highest revenue generating category
-Western Europe leads in profitability
-Sales peak consistently in Q4 (October to December)
-Copiers and Chairs are the top two revenue drivers
-Consumer segment leads in both sales and profit
-Discount above 30% consistently results in losses
