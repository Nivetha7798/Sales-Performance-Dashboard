# Sales-Performance-Dashboard

## Overview
Analyzed 51,290 global sales records to identify key business insights using Python.

## Business questions answered
-Which product category generates the highest revenue?
-Which regions are the most profitable for the business?
-Which month drives the highest sales volume throughout the year?
-Which specific product sub-categories contribute most to total revenue?
-Which customer segment delivers the best sales and profit performance?
-Does offering higher discounts actually increase or decrease profitability?

## Tools used
- Python, Pandas, Matplotlib

## Key insights
## Sales by category
-Technology is the highest revenue generating category
-Furniture and office supplies follow closely behind
-All three categories show relatively balanced sales distribution
## Total profit by regions
-Western Europe leads in profitability
-Significant profit gap exists between top and bottom regions
-Some regions generate high sales but low profit margins
## Sales Trend over time
-Sales peak consistently in Q4 (October to December)
-February shows the lowest sales dip across the year
-Clear upward trend in second half of the year
## Top 10 Sub categories
-Copiers and Chairs are the top two revenue drivers
-Technology sub categories dominate the top positions
-Significant drop in sales after top 3 sub-categories
## Sales vs Profit by Segment
-Consumer segment leads in both sales and profit
-Corporate segment shows strong sales but thinner margins
-Home office has lowest colume but relatively healthy profit ratio'
## Discount vs Profit
-Discount above 30% consistently results in losses
-0 or minimal discounts generate the highest profits
-Heavy disocunting is the biggest threat to profitability
